Availability uses Pout=1-exp(-10^(-M/10)) with residual fade margin M and an ideal Rayleigh envelope. This is a statistical single-link estimate, not measured uptime. Topology redundancy assumes independent radio path outages; shared power, structures, weather, interference and site failures are excluded. Fresnel and earth-curvature checks use flat-ground assumed heights, not terrain data. Rain k=2e-05 and alpha=1.000 are illustrative, not a verified ITU-R lookup. Gas attenuation 0.010 dB/km is assumed; fixed-site Doppler is near zero (100 km/h comparison 83.4 Hz).
